FAQ

good questions.

Anything else, email hello@highmind.com.au. A human reads it.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. highmind works from your iPhone on day one. A watch or any wearable that syncs to your iPhone gives it richer sleep and heart data, so your Focus Score gets sharper.

What does it cost?

It's free, and everything is free while we launch.

What does highmind actually read?

Sleep, heart and movement data from your phone and watch, only with your permission. Plus how your day is going, how each reset lands, and what you choose to tell Neo.

Who can see my data?

You. It's never sold, never shared with your employer, and you can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. highmind is a performance tool, not a medical one. If you're struggling, please talk to a GP or mental health professional.

Is it on Android?

iPhone only for now.
