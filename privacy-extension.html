highmind for Chrome: privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026. This covers the highmind browser extension. The highmind iPhone app has its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The only exception is anonymous usage stats (which features you use, never what you do in them), which you can turn off.
  • We record which site is in front (for example docs.google.com), never full web addresses or what is on the page.
  • Everything is deleted automatically after 30 days, and you can delete it any time in Settings.

What the extension records, on your computer

  • Site names of your active tab, once a minute, plus how often you switch. This is how your Focus Score and patterns are worked out.
  • Your lock-ins, resets and to-do list, and your answers to Neo's check-ins (for example "feeling it?").
  • Your calendar, only if you turn it on. Today's meeting times and titles, read from a Google Calendar or Outlook tab you already have open, or from a calendar link you paste.
  • Your inbox, only if you turn it on. The sender name and subject line of today's unread emails from people, read from your open Gmail tab. Never the email body. A typing signal (not what you type) tells writing apart from reading.
  • Desktop apps, only if you install the optional Mac helper. The name of the app in front (for example Figma), never its content.

What leaves your computer

  • Anonymous usage stats (on by default, easy to turn off). To learn which features are useful, the extension sends events such as "opened the widget", "started a 25 minute lock-in" or "rated Neo's message as useful", tagged with a random install id that isn't linked to you. These go to our analytics provider (PostHog). They never include site names, page content, calendar or email content, anything you type, your name or your email. Turn them off in Settings, under Privacy.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you sign in, Google tells us your name and email address so your account can be recognised. If you connect Google Calendar, today's events are fetched directly from Google to your computer.
  • Linking with the highmind phone app (optional, coming soon). If you link the same account, numbers only are synced through our database (Supabase): from the laptop, your Focus Score, focus minutes, switch count, meeting minutes and Neo's crash window call; to the laptop, your sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate from the phone. No site names, meeting titles or email content are ever synced.
  • Share your week (optional). If you choose to send it, a numbers-only summary you can read first is sent from your own email app to hello@highmind.com.au.

Your choices

  • The calendar reader, inbox reader, Mac helper, Google sign-in and phone link are all off until you turn them on, and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Settings lets you download everything the extension has stored, or delete it all.
  • Removing the extension deletes its data from your computer.

Limited use

highmind's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to show you your own focus, calendar and patterns, and is never transferred or sold to anyone else.
